Hi, I’m

Alex Koceyan

Alex Koceyan is an illustrator, designer, and muralist based in Metro Detroit. He works across a variety of visual styles, creating everything from illustrations and graphic identities to large-scale murals. Alex earned his BFA from Wayne State University, studying Painting and Drawing with minors in Illustration and Graphic Design.
